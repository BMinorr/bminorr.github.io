bBounce

by B Minor

bBounce is an app and a plugin (VST3, AU, CLAP) for music producers. It brings your bounces together from Samply and from your own cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive and others), lets you listen to them with lyrics, comments and stems, and sends them to artists as links or QR codes.

It runs on macOS, Windows, Linux, Android and iPhone.
