Privacy policy

bBounce · last updated 20 October 2026

bBounce has no servers of its own and no accounts of its own. It doesn't collect analytics, doesn't show ads and doesn't sell or share your data. Everything it keeps stays on your device, or in the cloud accounts you connect yourself.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, playlists, tags, statuses, contacts, message templates and cached covers.
  • The sign-in tokens of the services you connect (Samply, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, SoundCloud and others). They are used only to talk to those services for you.
  • A crash report, if the app closes unexpectedly. It is never sent automatically: the next start offers to copy it or to open a GitHub issue with it, and you choose.

Google Drive

When you connect Google Drive, bBounce asks for two permissions:

  • drive.file: access only to the files and folders bBounce creates or you open with it. bBounce cannot see the rest of your Drive.
  • Your email address: shown in the app so you know which account is connected.

bBounce uses this access to upload the tracks you choose to send, to create share links for them, and, if you turn on sync, to keep a small settings file (.bbounce-sync.json) in its own folder so your devices stay in step. Data from Google is used only for these features, is not transferred to anyone else, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models. bBounce's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect at any time from bBounce's settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Other services

When you send a track, bBounce uploads it to the service you picked (for example Samply, Dropbox, OneDrive, SoundCloud, Pixeldrain or Gofile). Lyrics sent with a link may be stored as a paste on dpaste.com so the web player can show them. What those services do with the files is covered by their own privacy policies. bBounce only sends what you ask it to send.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling bBounce removes everything it keeps on the device. Files it uploaded stay in your cloud accounts until you delete them there.
